## Things I'd love to talk about

Not every interesting conversation needs a business case. Client work is one reason to write. Here are a few others I'm always glad to find in my inbox.

- **Mentoring & knowledge sharing**: developers, students and early-stage founders working through a technical problem, an architecture choice, a tooling mess or a career question. Fifteen years of mistakes are more useful shared than hoarded.
- **Advisory & brainstorming**: architecture, developer experience, technical strategy, AI-assisted development, engineering workflows, or a second opinion on the decision you keep going back and forth on. A conversation to think something through, not a consulting engagement in disguise.
- **Non-profit & community**: open-source, educational, community and non-profit initiatives where my experience could genuinely help. I can't say yes to all of them, so tell me what you're building and where you think I'd fit.
- **Fun & experimental projects**: the weird ones. Creative coding, self-hosting, local AI, automation, developer tools, hardware hacking, DIY builds, experiments with no business plan whatsoever. If your message opens with *"this is probably a silly idea, but..."*, it already has my attention.
- **Talks & community**: meetups, workshops, technical discussions and community events where practical experience beats a polished slide deck. I've talked terminals in public before and would happily talk shop again.

Got an idea? [Send me a few lines](mailto:{{ site.email }}). Half-formed is fine. Unconventional is welcome.
